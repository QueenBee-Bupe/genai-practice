## 1. Project Overview

Surviving Autoimmunity is an AI-powered health management concept designed to help women living with complex autoimmune conditions organize fragmented medical information and better understand their health experiences over time.

The project addresses the difficulty of tracking symptoms, laboratory results, medications, appointments, and lifestyle factors across multiple healthcare providers.

## 2. Intended Users

Women living with diagnosed or suspected autoimmune conditions who need a more organized way to document their health histories and communicate with healthcare professionals.

## 3. Working Prototype

**Application:** https://surviving-autoimmunity.replit.app/care

The prototype demonstrates the intended patient-facing experience. The current implementation of AI functionality, data persistence, and individual features requires further verification and documentation.

## 4. Intended Role of AI

The proposed AI functionality would organize patient-provided information into understandable longitudinal summaries.

Inputs may include symptoms, medication histories, laboratory results, and relevant lifestyle information.

Intended outputs include structured timelines, summaries of reported changes, and information patients can review before discussing it with healthcare professionals.

These are intended capabilities, not claims that all features are currently implemented.

## 6. Evaluation Plan

Five distinct scenarios will be evaluated:

1. Typical: Record a new symptom.
2. Typical: Review existing health information.
3. Challenge: Enter incomplete or ambiguous information.
4. Invalid: Submit an empty or unsupported input.
5. Risk: Examine whether the interface encourages unsupported medical conclusions.

For each test, the expected behavior, actual observed result, pass/partial/fail status, and next step will be recorded.

**Test results:** Pending execution and documentation.

## 7. Risk and Ethics

A central risk is that patients may interpret AI-generated summaries as verified medical conclusions.

The intended safeguard is patient review of generated information, explicit communication of uncertainty, and clear separation between organizing health information and providing medical advice.

Implementation and effectiveness of these safeguards have not yet been verified.

## 8. Known Limitations

- Clinical accuracy and reliability have not been established.
- The application has not been validated for medical decision-making.
- Privacy and security controls require verification before real patient information is used.
- AI implementation status and feature behavior require further technical documentation.
- The current prototype has not been revised for this milestone.
